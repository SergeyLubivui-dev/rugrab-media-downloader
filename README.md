# RuGrab

### Your favorite videos, ready when you are.

RuGrab is a free video and audio downloader for Windows, macOS, and Android. Paste a link, choose from the available formats, and save media for offline use.

**[Visit the website](https://rugrab.ru/)** · **[Downloads](https://rugrab.ru/releases/)** · **[Report a problem](../../issues/new/choose)**

## One link. A few clicks. Done.

- **Download video and audio** from supported public sources, including YouTube, RuTube, VK Video, OK, TikTok, and Dzen.
- **Pick the format and quality** available for your link.
- **Keep track of downloads** and find your saved files in the app.
- **Work across devices** with free apps for Windows, macOS, and Android.

Availability depends on the source, the individual link, region, and app version. Private, restricted, or protected content may not be available.

## How it works

RuGrab is built around a custom download engine developed by our team. It brings link processing, download management, and media handling together in a straightforward experience. App packages may also include third-party components for specific tasks; their applicable licenses and notices accompany the corresponding distribution.

Learn more in [Technology](docs/TECHNOLOGY.md).

## Community

Have a bug report or an idea? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose). Please include your operating system, app version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Don't share passwords, access tokens, private links, or personal information.

This is RuGrab's public information and community repository. The app and its download engine are distributed as compiled software; their source code is not published here. A public repository does not make the app open source.

Please download only content you own or have permission to save, and respect applicable laws and platform terms.
