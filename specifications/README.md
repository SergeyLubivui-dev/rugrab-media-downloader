# RuGrab specifications

## Supported sources

RuGrab can download publicly available video and audio from supported sources, including YouTube, RuTube, VK Video, OK, TikTok, and Dzen. Availability depends on the individual link, region, platform changes, and app version. Private, restricted, or protected media may be unavailable.

## Output and quality

The app displays the formats and quality options available for each link. Video can be saved as MP4; audio-only output may be MP3 or M4A/AAC depending on the platform and bundled media tools. The available resolution, bitrate, and final format depend on the source and the app build. Check the options shown in the app before downloading.

## Download speed

Speed varies with the source server, network connection, device, file size, and other traffic. Some downloads use parallel segments. Processing or combining streams after downloading can add time. There is no guaranteed download speed; the app displays progress and speed where available.

Download only content you own or have permission to save, and respect applicable laws and platform terms.
