# Technology

RuGrab is designed to make saving supported video and audio straightforward across Windows, macOS, and Android.

## A custom engine

The RuGrab team develops its own download engine to support the app's experience across platforms. The engine handles the download workflow and works with the app to present available options and progress. This repository shares product information and does not publish the engine's source code or internal implementation.

## Formats and sources

Depending on the source and the media, RuGrab can offer different video qualities or audio options. Available choices can change as platforms update their services. Some content may be inaccessible because of privacy settings, region restrictions, protection, or other limitations.

## Third-party software

Some distributions use third-party components for media processing or compatibility. Consult the license and attribution notices included with each official release for the components in that release. The availability of a component can vary by platform and version.
